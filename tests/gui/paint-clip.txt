# The desktop's clipboard in Paint (apps/paint; phase E, docs/v1/apps/paint.md): what Copy takes is put on GLOS's
# clipboard as a picture, and Paste takes the picture that is there, whoever put it. A stroke drawn, selected and
# copied; then the clipboard is given words in the picture's place (the script's clip, as another program's
# copy), and Paste finds no picture; copied again and pasted at another selection's corner, as a layer of its own.
# (Each step is waited for by what Paint says of it: the words are put on the clipboard from outside, at once,
# and a copy Paint had not got to yet would come after them.)
run PAINT.EXE
wait "Untitled - Paint"
drag "Untitled - Paint" 70 89 120 139
key s
drag "Untitled - Paint" 70 89 120 139
key ctrl+c
said PAINT copy 51x51
clip words and no picture
key ctrl+v
said PAINT paste none
key ctrl+c
said PAINT copy 51x51
drag "Untitled - Paint" 200 99 220 119
key ctrl+v
key p
shot pasted "Untitled - Paint"
key alt+f4
wait "Paint"
key alt+n
expect PAINT select 10,10 51x51
expect PAINT copy 51x51
expect PAINT paste none
expect PAINT paste 51x51 at 140,20
expect PAINT end
