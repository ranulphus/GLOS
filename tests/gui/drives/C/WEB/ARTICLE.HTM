A Quiet Day in the Village

Nothing happened in the village today, which the people who live there say is just how they like it.

The baker opened at seven, as she has every day for thirty years, and the bus came at a quarter past eight. By noon the square was warm and the cat had found its usual place on the wall.

The afternoon

In the afternoon a light rain fell for ten minutes and stopped. Nobody minded; the gardens wanted it.
