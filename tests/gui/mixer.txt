# The Mixer (apps/mixer; phase F, docs/v1/apps/mixer.md) on the test's own numbers (MIXER -demo: a card that is
# always there, streams that come and go at F9): the master, the card's channels and the input recorded; a
# program's strip when it begins to play, a second, the first gone; the master turned down by the keyboard, a
# stream and a channel of the card muted by the mouse (the card takes its levels in steps: 248 for 255), another
# input chosen. (A pause before the pictures that follow a level's fall: a meter holds its peak a second and a half.)
run MIXER.EXE -demo
wait "Mixer"
shot none "Mixer"
key f9
said MIXER streams 1 PLAYER
shot one "Mixer"
key f9
said MIXER streams 2 PLAYER SKIING
shot two "Mixer"
key down
said MIXER master 75 mute 0
click "Mixer" 473 195
said MIXER stream SKIING volume 128 balance -128 mute 1
click "Mixer" 143 195
said MIXER channel Wave 248,248 mute 1
click "Mixer" 228 221
key down
key enter
said MIXER input Line in
pause 2000
shot set "Mixer"
key f9
said MIXER streams 1 SKIING
pause 2000
shot second "Mixer"
key esc
gone "Mixer"
expect MIXER card yes channels 6
expect MIXER master 75 mute 0
expect MIXER input Line in
expect MIXER end
