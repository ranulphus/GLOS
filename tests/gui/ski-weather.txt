# Fall Line's weather (apps/ski; phase F, docs/v1/apps/skiing.md): the demonstration held on mountain 7 at step
# 240, and the Weather menu's in turn there: snowfall (flakes placed by the run's own steps), fog (the slope ahead
# whitened, the more the further), night (dark but for the skier's lamp); then clear again.
run SKI.EXE -demo 7 -grade 2 -until 240 -speed 4 -table C:\\OUT\\SKIWX.DAT -day 20261121
wait "Fall Line"
said SKI ready seed=7 grade=2 mode=0 day=0
said SKI started watching
pause 1500
said SKI at 240 x=435 y=827 speed=232 score=0 gates=1/0 falls=0
key alt+w
key s
said SKI weather 1
shot snow "Fall Line"
key alt+w
key f
said SKI weather 2
shot fog "Fall Line"
key alt+w
key n
said SKI weather 3
shot night "Fall Line"
key alt+w
key c
said SKI weather 0
key alt+f4
gone "Fall Line"
expect SKI end
