# Paint

Paint draws pictures and changes them. It opens what [Images](IMAGES.MD) opens and saves PNG, BMP, JPEG and
PCX.

## Drawing

Choose a tool by its button or its letter, and two colours from the palette. The left mouse button draws in
the first colour, the right button in the second.

| Key | Tool |
|---|---|
| P | Pencil |
| B | Brush |
| E | Eraser (paints the second colour) |
| L | Line |
| R | Rectangle |
| O | Ellipse |
| F | Fill |
| I | Colour picker: takes a colour from the picture |
| S | Select a part of the picture |
| T | Text |

- **[** and **]** make the tip thinner and wider.
- **Tools, Shapes Filled** fills rectangles and ellipses.
- **Esc** lets go of what is selected.
- **+** and **-** zoom, **0** fits the window, **1** is the real size. The middle mouse button moves the
  picture in the window.

## Editing

Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Redo (Ctrl+Y) go back and forward step by step. Cut, Copy and Paste (Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C,
Ctrl+V) work on what is selected; Ctrl+A selects everything.

## The whole picture

- **Image:** turn it, flip it, change its size (Resize), give it more or less room (Canvas Size), keep only
  what is selected (Crop to Selection).
- **Adjustments:** Invert (Ctrl+I), Black and White, Sepia, Brightness and Contrast.
- **Effects:** Blur, Sharpen, Emboss, Find Edges, Pixelate.

## Layers

A picture can be several layers, one over another, listed at the right: draw on one without touching the
others. The **Layers** menu makes a new one (Ctrl+Shift+N), copies, deletes and moves them, merges one into
the one below, hides one, and sets how much shows through. Saving makes one picture of them all.
