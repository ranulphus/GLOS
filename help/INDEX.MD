# Welcome to GLOS

GLOS is a desktop for DOS PCs: windows, a Start menu and a task bar, programs of its own, and your DOS programs
and games beside them. It starts from DOS and gives the PC back to DOS when you leave it.

This guide tells how to use it. Press **F1** in any of GLOS's programs to open that program's page. The
contents are at the left; **Ctrl+F** looks for words in every page.

## In a few lines

- **Start a program:** the Start menu (the GLOS mark on the task bar, or Ctrl+Esc), or an icon on the desktop.
- **Start a DOS program or a game:** double-click it in [Files](FILES.MD), or keep your games in
  [Games](GAMES.MD). A game has the whole screen until it ends.
- **Change how GLOS looks and behaves:** the [Control Panel](CONTROL.MD).
- **Leave GLOS:** Start, Power, Exit GLOS. You are back at DOS's prompt.
