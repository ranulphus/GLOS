## What they share

- **F1** opens the program's page of this guide.
- Menus open with Alt and their underlined letter; most commands have a key of their own, shown beside them
  in the menu.
- **Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V** copy, cut and paste; what is copied in one program can be pasted in another.
- **Open** and **Save As** dialogs are the same everywhere: type a path, or pick a drive and folder.
- Each program keeps its own choices in a small file beside itself.
