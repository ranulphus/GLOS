# Images

Images shows pictures: PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF (the first picture of a moving one), TGA, PCX and PNM files. It
changes nothing on the disk: to change a picture, open it in [Paint](PAINT.MD) (the E key).

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+O | Open a picture |
| PgDn, PgUp | The folder's next picture, and the one before |
| F | Fit the window (a small picture is not made bigger) |
| 1 | Its real size |
| + and - | Larger and smaller, from an eighth to eight times |
| Arrows | Move over a picture larger than the window |
| L, R | Turn left, turn right |
| S | Slideshow: the next picture every four seconds; Esc stops it |
| F11 | The whole screen; Esc, F11 or Enter comes back |
| T | A strip of the folder's pictures along the bottom |
| Alt+Enter | The picture's details |
| E | Open it in Paint |

Turning and flipping (the Image menu) change what you see, not the file.
