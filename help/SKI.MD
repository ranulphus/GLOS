# Fall Line

A downhill skiing game: a slope seen from above, a skier, and a mountain made new from a number each time.
The fall line is the way straight down a slope. A fall costs you time, never the run.

## Skiing

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| Enter or Space | Start the run |
| Left, Right (or A, D) | Turn the skis; in the air, turn yourself |
| Up (or W) | Tuck: faster, and slower to turn |
| Down (or S) | Snow-plough: brake |
| Esc or P | Pause, and go on |
| F2 | The same mountain again |
| F3 | A new mountain |

**A game pad** steers too: the stick turns the skis, forwards on it (or the first button) tucks, back on it
(or the second button) ploughs. **The mouse** steers once you move it over the slope: the skier turns
towards the pointer, the left button tucks and the right one ploughs. A key or the pad takes over from the
mouse again.

- The nearer the skis point to straight down, the faster you go. Skis across the slope stop you.
- **The snow:** the piste in the middle is fast; the powder at its sides is slow; in the forest it is slower
  still. Pale blue patches are **ice**: fast, and the skis hardly hold, so turn before it.
- **A tree, a rock, a fence, a pylon of the chair lift, the hut or another skier** is a fall. You are up again in a second or two, and nothing can
  knock you down in the first moment after.
- **A ramp** taken fast throws you into the air. Turn while you are up there: land a half turn or a whole one
  for points, but land part way round, or with the skis across the slope, and you fall. Moguls, the small
  bumps, slow you, or at full speed lift you a little.

## Three kinds of run

Chosen in the **Mountain** menu:

- **Slalom:** go between each gate's red and blue poles, against the clock. A gate missed adds five seconds.
  Ski a mountain again (F2) and your best run there skis beside you as a grey shape: a ghost to beat.
- **Free run:** the whole mountain for points: for the way you make without a fall, for time in the air,
  for turns landed, and for passing things closely.
- **The Mountain of the Day** (F4): the same mountain for everyone that day, with tables of its own.

and **four grades**, as on a real mountain: green, blue, red and black. Each is steeper and narrower than the
one before, with more in the way, and from blue up there is ice.

## Mountains have numbers

Every mountain is made from a number, shown at the right. **Mountain, A Mountain's Number** (Ctrl+G) takes
you to one by its number: give a friend the number and you both ski the same mountain.

## The tables

The best ten runs of each grade and kind are kept, with names: **Game, Best Runs** (Ctrl+B). A slalom is
ranked by its time, a free run by its points.

## Watching

**Game, Watch the Careful Skier** (F9) shows the mountain skied without a fall: a way down that always
exists. It is not the fastest way.

Leave the game at the top of a mountain for half a minute and the careful skier starts by itself, going on
from mountain to mountain; any key or click gives the game back where it is.

The **Skier** menu chooses who goes down the mountain (a skier, a racer, a snowboarder or a sit-skier)
and the colour of their jacket. The **Weather** menu brings snowfall, fog (less is seen ahead), night (only
your lamp lights the slope) or wind: gusts that push you across the slope, shown by streaks blown past. Steer
against them. **Game, Sound** turns the game's sounds off and on: the
skis' hiss tells you your speed and the snow you are on, with your eyes on the slope.
