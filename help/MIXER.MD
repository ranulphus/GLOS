# Mixer

The Mixer sets how loud things are. Changes are heard at once. Esc closes it.

- **Volume:** GLOS's own volume for everything, with a meter of what is playing.
- **Sound card:** the card's own levels, where it has them, and what it records from: the microphone, the
  line input, the CD, or all of them.
- **Programs:** a strip for each program that is playing now, with its volume, its balance between left and
  right, and Mute. Strips come and go as programs start and stop playing.

The loudspeaker on the task bar changes the main volume without opening the Mixer. More in [Sound](SOUND.MD).
