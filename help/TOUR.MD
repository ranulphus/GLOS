# The tour

A short tour of the desktop. Help shows it the first time the desktop comes on after GLOS is installed, and
again whenever you choose **Tour** on Help's Help menu. Each part below is one step of it; a step that
points at a part of the screen says which.

## Welcome to GLOS

GLOS is a desktop for DOS: windows, programs of its own, and your DOS programs and games as they always
were. This tour shows where things are. Enter goes on to the next step, Esc ends the tour. Where a step has
**Show Me**, it opens the thing itself.

## The Start button

*At the Start button.*

Every program, the settings and Help are here. Open it and type a few letters to find a program or a
setting by its name.

## The task bar

*At the task bar.*

A button for each window. Click one to bring its window to the front, or hold Alt and press Tab to go from
window to window.

## The notification area

*At the notification area.*

The volume, the network and messages from programs. Click the speaker for the volume; click it twice for
the Mixer.

## The clock

*At the clock.*

Double-click it to set the date, the time and the time zone.

## DOS programs and games

Double-click a program in Files, or type its name at the command line in the Terminal. A game has the whole
screen while it runs, as it would on DOS. The Games Library keeps your games in one place.

## Settings and Help

The Control Panel has every setting, and its Devices page every device. Press F1 in any program to open its
page of this guide.
