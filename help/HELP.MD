# Using Help

This is GLOS's guide. **F1** in any of GLOS's programs opens the guide at that program's page.

- **Contents** (Ctrl+T), at the left, lists every page. Choose one to read it.
- **Links** in a page lead to other pages: press one, or reach it with Tab and press Enter.
- **Back** (Alt+Left) and **Forward** (Alt+Right) go over the pages you have read; **First Page** is Alt+Home.
- **Search** (Ctrl+F): type one or more words and press Enter. The lines that have all of them are listed,
  each with its page; press Enter on one to read that page, where the first word is chosen on that line.
- Text in a page can be chosen with the mouse and copied with Ctrl+C.
- **Tour**, on the Help menu, shows [the tour](TOUR.MD) of the desktop: a few steps, each beside the part of
  the screen it is about. GLOS shows it by itself the first time the desktop comes on after it is installed.
- **The tip of the day:** one of [the tips](TIPS.MD) in a small window each time the desktop comes on, while
  its box **Show a tip when the desktop starts** is ticked (or **Tip of the day** on the Control Panel's
  Desktop page).
